# Old & New SDA Hymnal — Brand Concepts

## Hymnal Resonance

An open hymnal rises into a compact bank of organ pipes while the outer emerald bars echo a sound-wave meter. The central page gutter carries a flame-like mint gesture, giving the mark a devotional lift without relying on a literal religious symbol; small gold pipe details connect it to the app's chorus markers. The wordmark expands the idea with expressive, bookish lettering, an ampersand built around an open book and sound waves, and a structured `SDA HYMNAL` line. This is the most direct and immediately legible hymnal-app concept.

## Canticle Ampersand

The ampersand becomes the identity's hero: its broad ink stroke crosses three emerald staff lines and resolves into open-book pages at the base. A single gold square acts like a chorus marker or a final bar, providing a memorable accent at small sizes. The matching lockup gives the ampersand architectural scale between restrained literary forms for `Old` and `New`, with widely spaced hymnbook-inspired capitals below. This is the most ownable expression of the app's “Old & New” name.

## Canticle Dove

A rising dove is assembled from a staff-like wing, a musical phrase, and an open hymnbook, joining sacred symbolism and congregational song in one continuous gesture. Emerald carries the spiritual and musical elements while the small gold eye provides a warm focal point. In the lockup, the dove's staff sweeps directly into the custom lettering and the secondary capitals use small diamond page-cuts, making the symbol and name feel composed as one phrase. This is the most lyrical and explicitly sacred concept.

All supplied PNGs use transparent backgrounds. Logo marks are 1024×1024; wordmark canvases are 2000×400. The marks were designed with bold primary silhouettes for recognition at 26px, although the fine secondary details naturally simplify at that size.
