In plain language: hymn texts and bundled music work offline. There are no accounts or advertising. The app shares limited usage statistics to help improve the app and community hymn trends. Statistics sharing is enabled by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings. A saved choice to turn sharing off is preserved across app upgrades. We do not use these statistics for personalized content, advertising, or tracking across other apps. Optional YouTube videos require an internet connection.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Old and New SDA Hymnal mobile app for Android and iOS, developed by Rohan A. Smith - Exterbox ("we", "us"). The analytics practices below apply to versions that offer the "Share usage statistics" setting. Older versions without this feature do not send these app analytics.

2. Usage statistics and controls

While "Share usage statistics" is enabled in Settings, the app summarizes:

  • Hymns opened, hymnal edition, where a hymn was opened from, repeat opens of the same hymn within a calendar week, and favorite additions or removals. Repeat opens are recognized locally without a permanent device identifier.
  • Feature and screen usage, foreground time, playback attempts and outcomes, and broad timing measurements.
  • Search result-count categories, selection rank, refinements, and searches left without selecting a result. Search words and typed text are not sent.
  • Changes to reading, display, music, chord, and auto-scroll settings.
  • Predefined error categories for playback and other app features. Raw exception messages, stack traces, screenshots, audio, and session recordings are not sent.
  • App version, Android or iOS platform, Modern or Classic design, reporting week, weekday, and broad time-of-day categories.
  • Approximate country inferred from the network connection used to upload statistics.

We do not request names, contact information, accounts, advertising identifiers, hardware identifiers, GPS coordinates, contacts, or payment information for analytics. Hymnal usage may indicate religious interests; we do not infer or assign a person's religion or create personal profiles.

3. Offline storage and uploads

Statistics are accumulated in the app's private local database. The first upload is scheduled approximately seven days after analytics starts. About once a week, when the app is open and has an internet connection, it attempts to upload compact summaries. Uploads may be split into several batches. Failed uploads remain queued and retry with increasing delays. Background execution on an exact schedule is not required. Offline summaries are bounded in size and expire within approximately 90 days.

Random reporting identifiers are scoped to a week. Separate random batch identifiers prevent retries from being counted twice. These identifiers are not based on your name, hardware, or advertising ID. Submitted summaries are temporarily pseudonymous during processing; we do not describe them as fully anonymous merely because they contain no name. The server immediately combines accepted measurements into aggregate totals and retains opaque duplicate-prevention receipts rather than an installation's activity history.

The analytics database is excluded from Android and iOS device backups. Turning sharing off clears local analytics counters, queued uploads, and reporting identifiers. Your normal favorites, recents, and app settings continue to be stored separately on your device. The app does not upload your saved favorites list; with sharing enabled, future favorite changes contribute to usage counts.

4. Network access and approximate country

The app uses internet access for optional videos, analytics uploads while statistics sharing is enabled, and online content you choose to open. Analytics adds no location, camera, microphone, contacts, notification, or advertising-tracking permission prompt.

AWS CloudFront estimates the upload country from the connection's IP address. Country at upload time may differ from where earlier offline activity occurred; travel, VPNs, and mobile routing can affect it. We retain country codes in analytics, not raw IP addresses. Network providers necessarily process IP addresses to deliver requests. Our analytics application and configured access logs do not retain request bodies or raw IP addresses.

5. Optional videos and external websites

When you open a hymn video, the app loads an embedded YouTube player with privacy-enhanced mode enabled. YouTube/Google receives the network and playback information needed to provide the video and may process information under its own policies. Privacy-enhanced mode does not mean the video works offline or that no information reaches YouTube. You can use hymn texts and bundled music without opening videos. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Optional donation, ministry, and developer links open external websites. Those websites' privacy policies apply to your activity there. Our analytics does not collect payment information or your activity after leaving the app.

6. Children's privacy

Hymn reading and bundled music are available with statistics sharing off. We do not ask for a child's name, contact information, or age, and do not knowingly seek personal information from children. Parents and guardians can turn statistics sharing off in Settings and control access to optional online videos. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the address below.

7. Purposes, service providers, and retention

We use statistics solely to understand feature usage, improve usability and reliability, and produce aggregate hymn trends. We do not sell analytics data, provide it to advertisers or data brokers, use it to personalize content, or combine it with activity from other companies' apps.

Amazon Web Services processes uploads and hosts the analytics services in the United States (US East, Northern Virginia); CloudFront handles network requests through its distributed infrastructure. Developer reports are private. Any public community trends use aggregates and suppress groups with fewer than 20 contributing weekly identifiers. This minimum reduces exposure of small groups; it is not a guarantee that all combinations of information are anonymous.

The Statistics page presents the same community report to everyone: popular hymns, repeat opens, favorite additions, broad times of day, weekdays, and country highlights over completed reporting weeks. Some additional groups are withheld to reduce disclosure through subtraction. The app caches this public report for offline reading; viewing it does not require sharing your usage statistics. Private developer reports include more detailed aggregate feature usage and reliability counts and are protected by administrator sign-in through AWS Cognito.

Duplicate-prevention receipts are eligible for deletion after 120 days, and aggregate database statistics after 400 days. Exported reports are removed on the corresponding reporting-week retention schedule. Operational logs and temporary query results expire after 7 days. Routine automatic deletion can take additional time. No raw per-installation event log is retained on our analytics server.

8. Security

Uploads use HTTPS. Stored analytics is encrypted using AWS-managed encryption, database and report access is restricted, and database credentials are never included in the app. The collector accepts only a predefined measurement format and limits traffic and batch sizes. We use these measures to reduce risk, but cannot promise absolute security.

9. Your choices

Sharing is enabled by default, and you can opt out. You can enable or disable it in Settings under "Privacy & Statistics" without affecting hymn reading or music. Disabling stops new collection, cancels an active upload where possible, and clears pending local analytics. A request already accepted by the server may already have contributed to combined totals.

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the app's local analytics. It does not automatically remove aggregate totals already received by the server. Since we discard the link between a submission and its aggregate contribution, we generally cannot retrieve or separate an individual's contribution afterward. Contact us about privacy questions or applicable data rights; we do not require you to identify yourself simply to use the app.
